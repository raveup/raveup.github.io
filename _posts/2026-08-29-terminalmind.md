Another true collectable gift from Rave Up Records! "Recordings" is the definitive retrospective LP of songs by the seminal Austin, TX punk band Terminal Mind.  

It contains the newly mastered self-titled EP from 1979, two unreleased studio demos, the two songs included on the first "Live At Raul's" LP plus an unreleased outtake from the same session, and live recordings from club dates and outdoor festivals.  

The band was only active from 1978-1980 but influenced many well-known groups like **Butthole Surfer**s and **The Jesus Lizard**, as well as offering early opening gigs to The Big Boys and Sharon Tate's Baby, among many others.  

Band leader **Steve Marsh** went on to form the experimental psych rock group Miracle Room, and continues to perform and record provocative underground music today. Info can be found at [magicmarshroom](https://www.magicmarshroom.net/) and his discography is located on (Bandacamp)[https://magicmarshroom.bandcamp.com/]!
